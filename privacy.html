TESSA Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026 · Applies to the TESSA desktop app for Windows, including the Microsoft Store version.

In short: TESSA does not collect, store or send any personal information. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no telemetry and no advertising. Your documents stay on your computer and are never uploaded to us or anyone else.

Who we are

TESSA is developed by Adithyan Lalu, an individual developer. Contact: adhy092018@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. TESSA has no servers of its own and does not send your documents, file names, usage data, crash reports or device identifiers anywhere.

What stays on your computer

WhatWhere
Your projects and documentsWherever you save them (default: Documents\TESSA)
Build output and file historyA .tessa folder inside each project
Settings and your personal spelling dictionaryThe app's settings folder (for example %APPDATA%\TESSA)
Error logsThe app's log folder. Logs are never sent automatically; you can open the folder with Open Log Folder in the app menu and choose to share a log with us if you ask for support.

You can delete any of this at any time by deleting the files or uninstalling the app.

When TESSA connects to the internet

TESSA works offline. It only uses the internet in these cases, and never to send your documents:

WhenWhat is sentTo whom
A document needs a LaTeX package that is not on your computer yet (usually only the first time)A request for the package filesThe package server of the open-source Tectonic project. The content of your document is not sent.
You choose Download common packages for offline useRequests for common LaTeX packagesThe Tectonic package server
You use Import a reference and type a DOI or arXiv IDOnly the DOI or arXiv ID you typeddoi.org or arxiv.org, which return the BibTeX entry
You click a web linkThe link is opened in your web browserThe website you clicked

These services receive your IP address, as any website does, and have their own privacy policies. TESSA does not add any identifier to these requests.

This website

The TESSA website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking. To show it, your browser contacts the web host (Vercel), Google Fonts (for the typefaces) and jsDelivr (for the 3D graphics library); downloads come from GitHub. Like any web server, these services see your IP address and keep standard access logs under their own privacy policies.

Children

TESSA does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date at the top will change.
